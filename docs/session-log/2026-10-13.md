# 2026-10-13: the engine, from an empty repository

## Read first

The predecessor pipeline (a single-account Hebrew slideshow bot with an approval desk) was read in the order asked: its README and editorial brief, the desk's README, then render, deck, posts, publish, schedule, slate, trends, metrics, formats, images, verify, the tells, voice and review modules, and the desk's web server, login and stylesheet. What carries over and what changes is in `docs/PLAN.md`.

## Phase 1: core

Done:

- `src/core`: paths under `$OF_HOME`, a tiny `.env` loader, the network door with its allowlist, a clock that tests can pin.
- `src/db`: SQLite through `node:sqlite`, one migration with every table (accounts, sources, documents, posts, slides, facts, steps, images, metrics, formats, spend, edits, audit, settings, users, onboarding, trend_formats, summaries).
- `src/schedule`: Israel's wall clock, sunset (USNO algorithm), the Shabbat window from Friday's candle lighting to Saturday's nightfall, per-account slots with dropped slots named.
- `src/packs`: loading and validation, including the cross-pack brand checks.
- `src/sources`: RSS/Atom, web pages, JSON and Wikidata APIs, recordings; every document stored with a hash.
- `src/llm`: Claude through the official SDK (handed the network door as its fetch), a recorded client, one `askJson` that guards the budget before a call and records the cost after.
- `src/budget`: the per-account and global monthly ledger.
- `src/voice`: the Hebrew tells, invented experience and fake proof, the bot-score reviewer with an offline heuristic.
- `src/verify`: facts against stored documents, the pre-render gate and its token.
- `src/rules`: pack rules, cross-promotion, the investment-advice compliance check.
- `src/images`: Pexels, Unsplash, Commons, licence allowlist, blocked queries.
- `src/render`: five templates with two variants each, Chromium with the network blocked, the font guard, shrink-to-fit, a measured scrim on photo covers.
- `src/pipeline`: the whole post, every step written to `steps`.
- `examples/packs/demo`: a fictional brand on reserved `example.*` domains, with six recorded documents and drafts.
- `npm run dry -- --pack demo --count 5`: 5 of 5 rendered in 17 seconds, about $0.044 a post (estimated).
- 62 tests, every hard rule so far.

Decided (details in `docs/decisions.md`): three dependencies; `node:sqlite`; one network door; Shabbat from the real sunset; recorded clients; templates in the engine and chosen by packs; the gate's token; investment advice as an engine check.

## Phase 1, finished: the first real pack

The first real account's pack was written in the private folder: researched
sources (vendor help centers and release notes, an expert's blog, established
publications), a voice guide, a theme, formats and rules, and recordings fetched
from the sources with `npm run record`. Its dry run renders 7 of 7. Running real
content through the checks found four engine bugs (Hebrew share by letters, a
prefix split from a name in the cover's word count, an ambiguous gendered form,
a blocked document drafted again) and check:public caught a real account name in
a font-check string. All fixed before the commit.

## Phase 2: the website

Teder (תדר), the approval desk: login, Today, Post, Accounts, Results, Sources,
Settings. Server-rendered Hebrew RTL, phone first, light and dark, one small
script, strict CSP (no inline style or script anywhere). The dry publisher and
Zernio behind the same interface. The first screenshot pass found a sideways
scroll on the post page (grid children need `min-width: 0`), a bidi swap in
"1/10", English on the Settings page, a fit check that refused tall faces at any
size, and blank lazy images in full-page screenshots. All fixed.

Decided: the demo shows three fictional accounts, generated at run time from
`scripts/demo-extras.js` into `./.demo`; `examples/packs/demo` stays the one
tracked pack. Demo metrics are flagged `demo-seed` and the Results page says so.

## Phase 3: learning and autonomy

The runner (generation, the three modes, publishing at the slot), the rails,

the trend watcher (YouTube Shorts, the format and never the content, a kept

Next: onboarding (source discovery and sample posts), then the other two real

## Phase 4: onboarding

A new account from a seed (a niche, topics, an audience): Brave finds authority
sites, their feeds are found, the open-API catalog suggests structured sources,
YouTube shows what formats work in the niche, Claude scores every candidate with
a reason. The owner approves the list on the website; the engine writes the six
config files (a palette and a template no other account uses), validates them,
and renders five sample posts. The account cannot go live until the owner has
looked at all five. Without keys it says what it skipped and invents nothing.

## Phase 5: deploy, the other packs, the report

The systemd unit, the Caddyfile and a backup timer; the README. The two other
real packs were built in the private folder from researched sources, each with a
dry run of 5/5. Building them found five more engine bugs, all fixed with tests:

- `he()` found Latin runs after escaping and broke every `"` in a Hebrew
  abbreviation (ש"ח, צה"ל) into a visible `&quot;`
- a source name in Latin letters had its parenthesis moved by the Hebrew line
- a `>` inside an attribute value (Wikipedia does this) left tag pieces in the
  stored text, and citation markers stayed in quotes
- a count in Hebrew words ("חמש") was never compared with the quote
- the investment-advice check matched provider names inside ordinary words
  ("כלל" in "בדרך כלל")

Writing the report found one more: a Commons photo under CC BY or CC BY-SA was
used without its credit. The caption now carries the author and the licence, and
a photo with no author to credit is refused.

97 tests. Screenshots of every page regenerated from the demo. Nothing was
published and no real money was spent. docs/REPORT.md has where it stands.
