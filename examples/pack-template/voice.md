# הקול של החשבון

## מי מדבר

Who is talking: what they know, what they never claim, where their facts come from.

## למי

Who reads it, and what they want from a post.

## איך זה נשמע

- Register, sentence length, how the audience is addressed (always plural).
- What the account never does.
- How it sounds out loud: a long sentence beside a short one, the concrete detail first, everyday words. The engine already refuses the ad structures ("X: Y" headings, fragments in a row, "זה לא X, זה Y", "בלי X ובלי Y", teasers): name the niche's own clichés here.

## שורות טובות

- a good line, the way a person who knows the niche would say it to a friend
- another good line, with a real number, name or place in it
- a third good line

## שורות רעות

- a bad line, and why
- another bad line
- a third bad line
